# 260715-01 Group evidence windows by story: narrative assignment, per-story cards, post-card corroboration

## Problem Statement

Evidence windows are keyed only by (ticker, exchange_code, strategy, direction). Story identity —
"which underlying news event is this article about?" — does not exist anywhere in the evidence
accumulation model. Two failure modes follow:

1. **Distinct concurrent stories mix into one card.** Two different NVDA stories (a contract win
   and a product launch) usually resolve to the same (strategy, direction) pair, e.g.
   event_driven/buy, so their articles land in the same collecting window and the card is cut from
   the first `required_evidence_count` unique articles **in arrival order**. The resulting card's
   evidence — and the synthesis dossier built from it — describes two unrelated catalysts, and its
   risk/invalidation conditions can belong to neither.
2. **Continued coverage of one story produces duplicate cards.** When a window is satisfied it is
   marked `satisfied`, and the very next article for the same key seeds a fresh collecting window.
   Ten articles rehashing one press release within a few hours chunk into 3+3+3 → three
   near-identical cards (plus a fourth window left collecting). Cards #2 and #3 are
   pseudo-replication: they restate card #1 with staler timing, cost a synthesis LLM call each,
   pollute the review queue, and skew per-card metrics.

The evidence-count concept is broken in both directions: "3 articles of any kind on NVDA-buy" can
create a card from unrelated events, while "3 more outlets covering the same story" creates a
duplicate card instead of counting as corroboration of the existing one.

Downstream gates bound the trading harm (TradeExecutor rejects while a position is open or
working; already-priced suppression often rejects the later duplicates) but do not fix the
semantics, the wasted synthesis spend, or the review noise.

## Expected Behavior

Story identity becomes a first-class property of evidence windows and thesis cards, decided by a
constrained LLM assignment against stored story narratives — never by matching free-form labels.

### 1. Story narrative (seed-stable, append-only)

- When an article seeds a new window, ThesisBuilder builds the window's **story narrative** from
  that article's headline + its analysis `evidence_bullet_candidates` + `event_type`, and stores
  it on the window (`t_evidence_windows.story_narrative`).
- The narrative is **set once from the seeding article and never rewritten**. Later matched
  articles may at most append (e.g. their headline as an additional line); the seed portion is
  immutable so story identity cannot drift and article #5 still matches the window that articles
  #1–4 built.

### 2. Story assignment call

- Runs inside analysis persistence (after the per-article analysis, which supplies the incoming
  article's bullets), for each valid analysis, when story scoping is enabled.
- **Input:** the incoming article's headline + source summary (+ its analysis bullets), and a
  candidate list scoped to the same (ticker, exchange_code, strategy, direction):
  1. every `collecting` window's stored narrative, and
  2. every **unexpired** satisfied card's stored narrative (corroboration candidates, see §4).
- **Output (strict structured schema, constrained choice):** exactly one of
  `window:<id>` | `card:<id>` | `new_story`. Invalid output is treated as assignment failure (§5).
- Uses a cheap triage-class model, configurable independently of analysis/synthesis models; tokens
  are budgeted and accounted like triage tokens.
- When the candidate list is empty the call is skipped and a new window is seeded directly.

### 3. Story-scoped windows and cards

- Multiple collecting windows per (ticker, exchange_code, strategy, direction) are allowed; the
  `uq_evidence_windows_collecting` unique index is replaced accordingly (window identity within a
  key is the row itself; selection is by assignment result, not key lookup).
- Rolling per-article aging (`evidence_collection_max_minutes`), unique-article dedupe, freshness,
  already-priced, tradeability, and synthesis behavior are unchanged — they now just operate on a
  story-pure window.
- When a card is cut, the window's story narrative is copied onto the card
  (`t_thesis_cards.story_narrative`) and flows through card exports
  (`ThesisCardHistoryExporter`) and the monitoring UI.
- The card idempotency key derivation is unchanged (still article-id based).

### 4. Post-card corroboration (record-only, until card expiry)

- If the assignment call matches the incoming article to an **unexpired** satisfied card
  (same direction via the key scoping, same story via the narrative), the article is recorded as
  **corroboration**: a row in a new `t_card_corroborations` table
  (card_id, article_id, analysis_id, matched_at), exposed read-only in exports/UI.
- The card itself is **frozen**: evidence, confidence, validation status, and signal state are not
  modified, and no signal is (re-)published. The per-article analysis remains persisted as usual.
- Cards past `expires_at` are not corroboration candidates; a later article on the same story then
  seeds a fresh window (the story can legitimately re-fire after 6h).

### 5. Assignment failure fallback (fail open to merge)

- If the assignment call fails (transport error, retries exhausted) or returns schema-invalid
  output, the article is attached to the **oldest collecting window** for the key — i.e. exactly
  today's behavior — and the assignment is persisted with `assignment_source = 'fallback'` for
  audit. If no collecting window exists, a new window is seeded.
- Rationale: the merge failure mode preserves signal flow; the split failure mode (windows never
  filling) silently produces no cards.
- Every assignment (matched / new_story / corroboration / fallback) is persisted with enough
  context to audit story grouping decisions (incoming article id, candidate set, chosen target,
  model, tokens).

### 6. Configuration

- `THESIS_BUILDER_STORY_SCOPING_ENABLED` (default **false**): disabled ⇒ byte-for-byte today's
  behavior — no assignment calls, single window per key, no narrative columns populated.
- `THESIS_BUILDER_STORY_ASSIGNMENT_MODEL`, `THESIS_BUILDER_STORY_ASSIGNMENT_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS`,
  timeout/retries; documented in `thesis-builder/configuration.md`.
- Story-assignment tokens appear as their own category in API usage accounting.

### 7. Design-doc updates (at implementation time, per repo convention)

- `thesis-builder/behavior.md`: window keying gains the story dimension (§3 step 8), window
  satisfaction and card creation sections describe narrative seeding, the assignment call, the
  fallback, and post-card corroboration.
- `thesis-builder/data-model.md`: `story_narrative` on windows and cards, the
  `t_card_corroborations` table, the assignment audit record, and the card-history export field.
- `thesis-builder/configuration.md`: the new story-scoping settings.

### 8. Reprocess / backtest parity

- The reprocess path and Backtester regeneration run the same assignment step with the same
  configuration, scoped by `reprocess_run_id` as windows are today.
- The Backtester LLM cache (260708-02 pattern, already extended once for synthesis) also caches
  assignment prompts so repeated regeneration runs over the same window are free.

## Acceptance Criteria

- With story scoping enabled, two interleaved same-(strategy, direction) stories for one ticker
  produce two story-pure windows and two cards, each carrying the correct story narrative; no card
  mixes articles from both stories.
- With story scoping enabled, N follow-up articles on an already-carded story (before card expiry)
  produce zero additional cards and N corroboration rows on the existing card; after expiry, the
  same story can seed a new window.
- A window's seed narrative is byte-identical from creation through card cut, regardless of how
  many articles were appended.
- Every processed valid analysis has a persisted assignment record (window / card / new_story /
  fallback) with model and token attribution; fallback assignments are queryable.
- Assignment-call failure never drops an article: it lands in the oldest collecting window (or a
  new one) and is marked `fallback`.
- With story scoping disabled, behavior is byte-for-byte the current implementation (regression
  guard: zero assignment calls, one collecting window per key).
- Card exports and the monitoring UI show `story_narrative` and corroboration counts;
  TradeExecutor consumes signals unchanged.
- A regeneration backtest with story scoping enabled runs end-to-end; a repeat run hits the
  assignment cache (zero new assignment tokens); funnel counts reconcile
  (analyses → assignments → windows → cards → corroborations).

## Resolution

Status: resolved.

Root cause:
- Evidence windows were selected only by `(ticker, exchange_code, strategy, direction, reprocess_run_id)`, so unrelated same-key catalysts merged and later same-story coverage restarted a fresh window after the first card was cut.

Resolution:
- Added default-off story scoping controlled by `THESIS_BUILDER_STORY_SCOPING_ENABLED`.
- Added seed-stable `story_narrative` on evidence windows and copied it to thesis cards.
- Replaced the collecting-window uniqueness constraint with a non-unique collecting-key index so story-scoped mode can hold multiple same-key windows.
- Added a constrained story assignment client and repository flow that chooses `window:<id>`, `card:<id>`, or `new_story`; failures fall back to the oldest collecting window and persist `assignment_source='fallback'`.
- Added `t_story_assignments` audit rows and `t_card_corroborations` for post-card corroboration without mutating frozen cards or republishing signals.
- Wired story settings through live processing, reprocess, regeneration, and the backtester cached LLM client.
- Exposed `story_narrative` and `corroboration_count` in card exports and the Monitoring UI card list/detail.
- Updated ThesisBuilder and Monitoring UI design docs.
