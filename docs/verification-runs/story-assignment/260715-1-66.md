## What the pollution looks like

Pre-fix, story assignment was taken from the LLM on trust (260715-05). Retrieval surfaced a
same-ticker window/card, the LLM stamped `matched`, and thematically-adjacent but distinct
events were fused. The 10 `correct` matched cases are almost all **literal duplicate articles**
(same headline re-analyzed) or the same event via a second feed — i.e. the model only reliably
matched when the two texts were near-identical. Every genuinely distinct same-ticker event that
retrieval happened to surface was fused.

Seven cards published a signal on partly- or fully-off-story evidence:

| card | ticker | narrative (seed) | cross-story members that published | error ids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ffe63506` | NVDA | "dividend stock skyrocket after Aug 4" (junk seed) | Penguin Solutions memory; Buffett Dow-stock | 5, 6 |
| `089dba85` | GOOGL | EU antitrust-fine appeal | Alphabet $84.75B AI raise; Buffett Alphabet stake | 11, 15 |

| `d0fef169` | META | Meta Iris custom-chip manufacturing | Meta stock-rebound sentiment recap | 31 |
| `accdd8e0` | ORCL | Japan government-cloud contract | "Oracle down 60%" valuation piece (belonged in w2450) | 58 |
| `e3ba0bbe` | NVDA | Nokia/Nvidia AI-RAN partnership | Saturn Cloud/Lilac GPU (CS-0004) | 57 |

Post-card corroboration was the other big leak: 18 further same-ticker articles matched an
already-minted card that is a different event (e.g. six Vera-Rubin / NVDA-analyst articles
matched the junk `ffe63506` card; two more Vera-Rubin articles matched the *other* NVDA card
`e3ba0bbe` — the same underlying event scattered across two unrelated cards). These are
`card_minted` severity: they corroborate a bad published card without themselves minting a new
signal.

Cards `d8862236` (AVGO/Apollo, w2449), `85534902` (AVGO/Apple, w2452) and `63ef2cc5` (LLY,
w2453) were minted from cross-story-polluted windows but rejected downstream
(`already_priced` ×2, `untradeable_risk_box`) — contained by luck, not by the assignment layer.

### Notable cross-story swaps (retrieval + trust interacting)

- The identical "Apple to spend $30B with Broadcom" article was routed **correctly** to the
  Apple-partnership window 2452 (#46) but **incorrectly** to the Apollo-financing window 2449

- The "Oracle down 60%" article seeded its own window 2450 (#19, correct) yet a duplicate of it
  (#58) matched the unrelated Japan-cloud window 2439 and completed+published that card.
